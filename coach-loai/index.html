About

Loai founded ReShape Method to help women stop fighting their own bodies. With a team of coaches and nutritionists across two studios, ReShape has helped more than 1,200 people change their bodies, and 92% of members finish the programme.

His approach starts from one idea: a stuck plateau is usually a pattern, not a lack of willpower. He teaches women to eat enough, lift heavy, protect their sleep and work with the hormone changes of their 30s, 40s and 50s. He runs seminars on hormones after 30 and coaches members in the studio and through the ReShape Method app.

ReShape has been featured by the BBC, the Ipswich Star, the East Anglian Daily Times and Active Suffolk.
